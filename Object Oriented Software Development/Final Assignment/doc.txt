Classes and Objects: Character, Player, Enemy, Room, and Game are classes with their respective attributes and methods.

Inheritance: Player and Enemy classes inherit from Character.

Encapsulation: Attributes like _health, _attack_power, and _name are private and manipulated via methods like take_damage() and is_alive().

Polymorphism: Both Player and Enemy inherit the attack()  methods but can behave differently. (fixed and random)

Abstraction: is_alive, take_damage, attack methods are define as abstract method and implemented in child class.

Composition: Game object compose Player , Enemy and Room objects.
